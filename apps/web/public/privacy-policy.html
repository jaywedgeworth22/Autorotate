Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026

Overview

Autorotate ("we", "our", or "us") provides a zero-plaintext secret-rotation lifecycle management engine across web, macOS, and iOS platforms. Protecting your sensitive credentials and security posture is our core purpose.

Zero-Plaintext Architecture

Autorotate is designed with mathematical privacy and zero-plaintext guarantees:

  • No Plaintext Persistence: Secret material exists only in ephemeral memory during an active rotation execution and is never stored unencrypted in databases, disk logs, or analytics.
  • On-Device Encryption: Apple Keychain and secure hardware enclaves are used for local client storage.
  • Audit Logging: System audit logs store cryptographic hashes and state machine signatures, never raw secret payloads.

Information We Collect

We collect only telemetry required to operate rotation schedules (e.g. connector type, rotation frequency, status codes, and execution timestamps). We do not collect or inspect secret payloads.

Your Control & Data Rights

You can disconnect targets, revoke API tokens, or delete your account at any time through the Autorotate control dashboard or local companion apps.
